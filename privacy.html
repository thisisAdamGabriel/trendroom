Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Trendroom is a desktop application by Gabriel Axis that one creator installs on their own computer to manage their own TikTok account. This policy explains what Trendroom does with data.

Data Trendroom uses

  • From TikTok, with your permission (the scopes you allow when you sign in): your basic profile (display name, avatar, username), your profile stats (followers, following, likes, video count) and your public videos with their stats.
  • What you add yourself: your own video files, captions, notes, saved trend links, reminder lists and settings.

Where it is stored

  • Everything is stored only on your computer, in Trendroom's data folder.
  • Your TikTok sign-in tokens are kept in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager), never in plain files.
  • Trendroom has no server and no account system of its own. Gabriel Axis cannot see your data.

Where it is sent

  • Only to TikTok's official API (to read your own profile and video stats and to upload your own videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts), and to the AI model that runs locally on your own computer.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking services. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.

Your control

  • Sign out in Trendroom to revoke its access token; choose "delete local data" to remove that account's data from your computer.
  • You can also remove Trendroom's access at any time in the TikTok app's settings.
  • Deleting Trendroom's data folder removes all data it stored.

Children

Trendroom is not meant for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions: contact Gabriel Axis on GitHub at github.com/thisisAdamGabriel.
