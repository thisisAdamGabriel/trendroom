Trendroom

by Gabriel Axis

What it is

Trendroom is a personal Windows desktop tool for one creator to manage their own TikTok account, using only TikTok's official APIs:

  • Analytics of the creator's own profile and public videos (Display API).
  • Uploading the creator's own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API). The creator finishes and posts every video in the TikTok app.
  • Captions, hashtags and weekly reports written by an AI model that runs on the creator's own computer.
  • A trend board and reminder lists that the creator uses by hand.

Trendroom never posts publicly by itself, never follows, likes or comments for anyone, and does not collect data about other people.
